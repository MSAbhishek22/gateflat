GateFlat — the $WIF gate

A fail-closed gate for agent-drafted contract calls on Robinhood Chain. Secrets get blocked, unknown destinations get held, and every decision leaves a masked receipt plus a prepared onchain record. $WIF holders run the gate at half price.
